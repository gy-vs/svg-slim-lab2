Don't collapse a group whose inline style overrides a presentation attribute
of the only child, the presentation attribute has a lower priority than the
inherited style.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <g style="fill:red">
        <rect width="10" height="10" fill="blue"/>
    </g>
</svg>
