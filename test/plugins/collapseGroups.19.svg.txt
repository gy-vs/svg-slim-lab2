Don't collapse a group when the group style would override the child's own
presentation attributes, since inline styles have a higher priority than
presentation attributes. CSS property names are case-insensitive, so the
same applies to uppercase property names.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <g style="fill:red">
        <rect fill="blue" width="10" height="10"/>
    </g>
    <g style="FILL:red">
        <rect fill="blue" width="20" height="20"/>
    </g>
</svg>

@@@

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <g style="fill:red">
        <rect fill="blue" width="10" height="10"/>
    </g>

        <rect fill="blue" width="20" height="20"/>
    </g>
</svg>
