Collapse the group when the inline style and the child's presentation
attribute resolve to the same value, nothing visually changes.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <g style="fill:red">
        <rect width="10" height="10" fill="red"/>
    </g>
</svg>

@@@

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <rect width="10" height="10" fill="red" style="fill:red"/>
</svg>
