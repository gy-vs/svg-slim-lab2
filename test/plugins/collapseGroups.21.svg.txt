Don't collapse a group when the group style would override a stylesheet
rule matching the child, since inline styles have a higher priority than
stylesheet rules.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <style>
        rect { fill: blue; }
    </style>
    <g style="fill:red">
        <rect width="10" height="10"/>
    </g>
</svg>

@@@
