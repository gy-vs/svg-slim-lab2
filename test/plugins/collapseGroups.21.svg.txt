Don't collapse a group whose inline style overrides any presentation
attribute of the only child, including stroke-width.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
    <g style="stroke-width:2">
        <path d="M0 0L10 10" stroke-width="8"/>
    </g>
</svg>

@@@
